Covered workflows & remaining gaps

Coverage means a test exists for the stated scenario.

✓Has automated coverage

  • ✓
    Access control & exact DNS ownership

    Scope boundaries, authentication, client revocation, duplicate requests, and cleanup of only the owned TXT value.

  • ✓
    Certificate issuance & safe renewal

    Issue, renew, download, resume saved orders; a failed renewal keeps the previous certificate.

  • ✓
    Persistence & worker cleanup

    Encrypted credentials, credential rotation, expiry cleanup, saved-state recovery, and worker timeout termination.

  • ✓
    Administration & deployment

    Desktop/mobile browser flows; container install, restart, upgrade, and backup/restore with provider/challenge data.

!Partial coverage or not yet verified

  • ○
    Real DNS providers & upstream CA integration

    Cloudflare adapter HTTP and CA issuance are simulated. Live provider/staging checks and real CA account persistence are not verified here.

  • ○
    Abrupt crashes & storage failures

    No controlled crash after DNS changes but before saving; no forced config-file/database commit failure.

  • ○
    Protocol edge cases & richer restores

    ACME certificate revocation, account deactivation, and RSA auth are not hit by the measured CI suite. Certificate/key/account/order data needs upgrade/restore coverage.
