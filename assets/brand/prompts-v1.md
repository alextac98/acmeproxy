## Icon prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished, distinctive standalone app icon for ACME Proxy, a self-hosted DNS challenge gateway that keeps DNS credentials centralized and grants domain-scoped access. Design a bold geometric letter A that doubles as a gateway, with a simple routed path passing through its negative space. Elegant minimal silhouette, confident and memorable, exceptional legibility at favicon size. Flat vector-like graphic, crisp edges, restrained contemporary developer-tool identity. Choose a cohesive distinctive color treatment. Center the single mark with generous padding on a square canvas with genuinely transparent background. No text, no lettering beyond the abstract A mark, no mockup, no presentation board, no tiny network nodes, no padlock cliche, no shadows or 3D effects. Deliver one finished icon.
